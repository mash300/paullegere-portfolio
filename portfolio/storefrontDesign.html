WEB DESIGN · E-COMMERCE

Kricket Design

Designing and developing a responsive WooCommerce storefront for an independent automotive parts brand.


Year
2026

Roles
Product Designer
Web Designer / Developer
WooCommerce Implementation

Kricket Design is a small-batch automotive parts brand focused on independently developed products for enthusiast vehicles.

I designed and built a responsive WooCommerce storefront to support product sales, payments, inventory, taxes, shipping, and order fulfillment, while keeping the experience simple and manageable for a small business.

Kricket Design storefront introducing the product and primary path to purchase.


E-commerce Storefront


Problem

Kricket Design needed a simple, credible storefront that could support the sale of a small-batch automotive product without adding unnecessary complexity to the business.

The storefront needed to:

  • Clearly present product details and fitment information
  • Support responsive browsing and checkout
  • Handle payments, inventory, tax and shipping
  • Provide a professional purchase experience for real customers
Process

Storefront Structure

Planned a focused WooCommerce experience around a small product catalogue and a short path from product discovery to checkout.

Theme & Interface

Built the storefront using a WordPress child theme and custom Sass, with responsive layouts tailored for desktop and mobile.

Implementation

Configured WooCommerce to support live inventory, Square payments, taxes, shipping, and order fulfillment. I also implemented a custom WooCommerce plugin to calculate U.S. tariff and handling fees at checkout, helping make cross-border costs clearer before purchase.

Visual System


A lightweight visual system established consistent typography, colour, buttons, form elements, and interface styling across the storefront.



Responsive Storefront

The storefront was designed to remain clear and easy to use across desktop and mobile, with product information, calls to action, and checkout flows adapting to smaller screens.

Responsive storefront layouts across desktop and mobile.


Storefront Experience


Key storefront screens focused on clear product information, straightforward navigation, and a short path from product discovery to checkout.

Outcome
  • Responsive storefront designed around a focused product catalogue
  • Clear product details, fitment information, and purchase path
  • Integrated checkout supporting real customer transactions
  • Consistent visual experience across desktop and mobile

Reflection & Next Steps


  • Keep the storefront focused as the product catalogue grows
  • Continue refining product imagery and purchase information based on customer feedback
  • Expand the custom tariff solution with API-based rate data to reduce manual maintenance
  • Use real order and customer behaviour to guide future checkout and fulfillment improvements
